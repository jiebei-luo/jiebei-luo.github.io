## {{ site.name }}

{{ site.title }}, {{ site.institution }}

I study how people discover, evaluate, and use data, and how academic libraries can design more effective instruction and services around those behaviors.

My research connects data-seeking behavior, data and AI literacy, research data stewardship, and evidence-based library practice. I also use machine learning and natural language processing to study library services at scale.

### Current work

- **Data-seeking behavior:** Understanding how university students in the social sciences find and assess data, supported by an Institute of Museum and Library Services National Leadership Grant.
- **Multiple literacies:** Developing a multiple-literacy scale to strengthen academic library instructional services.
- **AI and library practice:** Studying AI competencies for librarians and the use of machine learning and NLP in library services.
